So, when the results came out, I was expecting to have to resit it.

This is not an isolated case, as I have passed all modules so far, and 2/3rds of the modules with a distinction. While doing a ton of extra stuff on top, including a postgraduate ML course at Imperial College London!

I think it's fair to say the current study break is going very well :)
