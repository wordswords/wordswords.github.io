The dust has settled on our move to Preston.

I am so happy to have it back in good working order! I had an excellent experience with <a href="https://www.leisurelakesbikes.com/">Leisure Lakes Bikes</a> in Preston, who I highly recommend and will be taking my bike to for any and all future services.

If you're interested in the new configuration, you can <a href="https://wordswords.github.io/my-bike/">read about it here</a>. It's definitely now the 'car replacement' vehicle I always wanted it to be!
