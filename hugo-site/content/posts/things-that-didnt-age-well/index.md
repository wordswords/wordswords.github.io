I remember seeing these '[EMachine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMachines)' PCs in the late 90s. By then, I had been using computers since 1986, starting with a [BBC Microcomputer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Micro).

I first got my own personal 'PC' in my bedroom in 1993, starting with an [IBM compatible 386](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I386).

I have been building all my own PCs since around 1999 right up to today, including [my current one](https://davidcraddock.net/my-work-computer).

Not a lot of people take the time to build their own computers these days, even in my line of work, but I really enjoy it, and love the level of customisation it offers.
